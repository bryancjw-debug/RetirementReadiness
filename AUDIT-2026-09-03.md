# CPF quiz and insurance release audit

Scope: RetirementReadiness only. Common Cents was not modified.

## Implemented

- Guided choices before amounts: mandatory contributions, optional three-account
  contributions, retirement-only cash top-ups, then optional insurance estimates.
- RSTU cash top-ups for employed, self-employed and non-working CPF members.
  Cash is actually debited; top-ups are limited by available cash and estimated
  statutory headroom. Unfilled amounts are disclosed, not credited to CPF.
- SA top-ups and attributed interest remain locked when SA closes at 55. RA
  headroom uses a persistent funding basis, not a declining CPF LIFE reserve.
- Self-employed mandatory MediSave uses annual NTI and the correct piecewise
  schedule. Three-account contributions share the $37,740 Annual Limit.
- Simple optional MediShield Life/IP, CareShield Life and supplement estimates.
  Age bands, withdrawal limits, payment end ages and assumed repricing are explicit.
  The $600 supplement limit is per insured, not a universal premium quotation.
- Actual MA-funded premiums and remaining cash expenses are shown by year.
  Mortgage amounts beyond available OA become cash expenses through the payoff age.
- Independent inputs and annual cost disclosures for each person in couple mode.
- Reused CPF model helpers and quiz component rather than parallel implementations.

## Audit corrections

- Prevented ordinary CPF drawdown before 55.
- Prevented CPF LIFE starting early when the projection horizon ends before 65.
- Retained unused retirement income in cash.
- Recorded insurance and housing cash costs even before retirement and highlighted
  pre-retirement funding gaps in individual results.
- Routed pre-55 MA overflow to SA only up to FRS, then OA; later retirement
  contributions use cohort FRS rather than a user-selected Enhanced target.
- Post-LIFE retirement inflows below FRS support additional estimated LIFE income.
- Updated the compatible Browserslist dependency chain: npm audit reports zero
  known vulnerabilities after the update.
- Removed a redundant failing deployment job. Main now validates; the established
  gh-pages branch remains the publication source. No protections were weakened.

## Explicit limitations

- This is an annual planning model, not CPF's monthly ledger or an official LIFE
  quotation. Age as at 1 January is approximated by entered age for SEP MediSave.
- Existing RA principal/interest history, historical top-up restrictions, CPFIS-SA
  investments and property eligibility are not collected. Exact top-up and lump-sum
  withdrawal eligibility must be checked with CPF. Basic-target modelling is not
  proof that a person qualifies for a property-backed withdrawal.
- RSTU relief is not credited as cash. The self $8,000 relief cap is shared with
  eligible MA cash top-ups; no MRSS grants or tax optimisation is simulated.
- Three-account SEP contributions are additional after mandatory MA is paid. An
  unpaid mandatory obligation offset by voluntary MA needs separate reconciliation.
- Enter regular cash savings net of mandatory/three-account CPF, but before the
  optional RSTU and extra insurance/housing cash deductions modelled here.
- An entered official LIFE monthly override stays fixed; obtain a new quote when
  changing future top-ups. Automatic increases use the app's approximate model.
- Hospital repricing defaults to 3% beyond age changes. Private IP age changes use
  the MediShield age curve as a proxy, not an insurer schedule. Riders are excluded.
- CareShield Life uses an editable current premium and illustrative 4% growth;
  subsidy, premium support, catch-up premiums, claims and waivers are not modelled.
- Supplement premiums are assumed level; the user must check actual policy terms.
- This audit is regression and responsive-layout verification, not actuarial
  certification or an exhaustive assistive-technology/device test.
